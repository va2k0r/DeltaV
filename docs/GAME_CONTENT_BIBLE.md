# Game Content Bible

DeltaV is a deterministic hard-sci-fi orbital strategy game about corporate warfare
beyond the protected Earth-Moon corridor.

Core design sentence:

```text
Hard-sci-fi orbital warfare reduced to its essential decisions.
```

Current v10 map identity:

```text
The planet is the gravity anchor. The moon is the tactical ground. The orbit is the war clock.
```

## Content Invariants

- One spendable resource: tritium as global operational delta-v.
- One ship type in v1.
- Ships control nodes; ships do not produce.
- Nodes produce.
- Factions are mechanically equivalent in v1.
- The first weapon is the missile.
- Defeat is loss of recoverable tritium mobility, not hit points.
- Earth and Moon are protected and not normal war nodes.
