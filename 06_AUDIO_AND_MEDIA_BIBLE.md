# DeltaV / AV Audio and Media Bible v5

## 1. Audio identity

AV operations music should pulse, not explode.

The most important gameplay/operations reference:

```text
Trium - Night Maneuvers (Carrier Command 2 OST, game speed)
```

Use as mood reference only. Do not copy melody, arrangement or recording.

Desired qualities:

- cold night operations
- restrained electronic pulse
- remote command
- radar/telemetry feeling
- dark mission already underway
- steady tension
- no heroic release

## 2. Playlist versus soundtrack

Reference playlist defines emotional target.

Soundtrack must be original, adaptive and functional.

It should not imitate Jóhann Jóhannsson, Hildur Guðnadóttir, Ben Frost, Reznor/Ross or Carrier Command 2 directly.

## 3. Gameplay music

Gameplay music should be loopable and low-fatigue.

It should support:

- planning
- transfer preview
- contested nodes
- missile countdown tension
- low reserve pressure
- operational nighttime mood

Avoid:

- epic orchestra
- cinematic stingers
- big drums
- synthwave nostalgia
- rock/metal
- heroic melody
- battle music
- cinematic climax

## 4. Sound design

Sound should be minimal and precise.

Important classes:

- UI ticks
- transfer preview lock
- invalid/out-of-range cue
- missile T countdown cue
- tritium reserve chart update
- ship micro-burns
- contested node micro-flashes
- cold white rupture for destruction/teaser

## 5. Contested node audio

If audible, contested node flashes should sound like distant cold micro-ruptures, not explosions.

No fireball boom.

No action-movie hit.

## 6. Teaser direction

Visual teaser:

- black void
- tiny low-tech modular ship with large solar panels
- blinding cold-white rupture
- two halves drift apart
- minimal AV monogram appears between them

Final teaser recommended duration: 10 seconds.

Flash sync point: 2.8 seconds.

## 7. External music prompt seed

Original instrumental cue for a hard-sci-fi orbital strategy game.

Cold operational electronic track for night maneuvers in deep space. Minimal, tactical, hypnotic. Slow pulsing synth bass, dry machine percussion, radar ticks, distant metallic transients, low pressure drones, restrained minor-key synth motif. The music should feel like remote command, telemetry, fuel scarcity, orbital calculation and silent corporate warfare.

No spectacle. No heroism. No space opera. No explosion. The cue should stay controlled and tense for gameplay, like a system running in the dark.

Loopable, instrumental, 95 BPM, dark electronic, minimal industrial ambient, cold military operations, deep space strategy.
